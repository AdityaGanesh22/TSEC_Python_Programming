## Typing fast

Imagine you are speedrunning your python course like a game... what special abilities would you like to have? Of course, you need to learn to type fast. We are not taught this explicitly, but it is presumed you know to type before you joined this course. I haven't told you anything about this during the course, but try experimenting it by yourself. Instead of wasting time on chess (an np-exponential hard problem, see this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YX40hbAHx3s) or other games, you can upskill yourself by practising typing games daily for at least 15 mins.

There is something known as words per minute (wpm). We write on paper between 13-23 wpm. While typing on the keyboard without any knowledge of _touch typing_, we tend to write 40 wpm. But you can go over 100+ wpm. Students who will be giving competitive exams like TOEFL, GRE, etc. will also have to type fast to write their essays. So better to start early.

- **Start here:** https://www.keybr.com/

- **Intermediate:** https://monkeytype.com/

- **Advanced:** https://play.typeracer.com/
